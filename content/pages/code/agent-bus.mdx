## What will be here

Two small pieces of Python, stdlib only, that every daemon in a fifty-six-agent fabric had in common:

- **The loop.** One `tick()` that drains an inbox, answers four directive kinds any agent understands, keeps a circuit breaker, audits every event, runs the agent's own work, and heartbeats. Subclasses overrode two methods and inherited the rest.
- **The bus.** Messages are JSON files delivered by atomic rename. Request and response with correlation ids and a timeout, no broker, no ports, no dependencies. Two machines shared one bus because the directory lived in iCloud.

And the part that matters more than the code: what it carried, what broke, the external review that followed, and what changed because of it.
